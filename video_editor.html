⏱️ Speed

🔊 Audio mix

Volume 100% se upar boost hai (Play dabane ke baad kaam karta hai). Fade clip ke start/end par lagta hai. Track vol us track ke saare clips par.

🎥 Stabilize (hilta video theek karo)

Sirf hilna (shake) theek hota hai, rotation nahi. Kinare dikhne se bachane ke liye thoda zoom/crop hota hai. Pehli baar analyse mein clip ki lambai jitna samay lagta hai.

🔲 Fit / Fill

Fill = video/photo crop hokar poori screen bharta hai (9:16 ke liye zaroori). Band = poora dikhta hai, kinare kaale.

🟩 Chroma Key (green screen)

Is clip ke neeche wale track par background clip rakho (Video 1 sabse upar hota hai). Pehle 🎯 se rang chuno, phir Similarity badhao jab tak green gayab ho.

✂️ Background hatao

Auto: clip ke kinaron ka rang background maana jata hai, jo kinare se juda hai wahi hatega. Saaf/ek-rang background par best. AI mode ko pehli baar internet chahiye (wasm ~10 MB + model ~0.3 MB, cache ho jata hai).
