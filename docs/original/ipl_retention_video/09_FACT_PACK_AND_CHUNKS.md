# 09 IPL 2027 Retention Preview - Fact Pack + 5 Chunks (16 Nov 2026)
Source (main): HT, "IPL 2027 retentions: What all 10 teams could do..." (Probuddha Bhattacharjee, updated 16 Nov 2026, 13:28 IST). Tumne poora text paste kiya.
Cross-check: Tribune, Express Tribune, Fanatik (wire), IANS, Business Today, Siasat, Mykhel (search se, 16 Nov).

**Format:** C (Release/Retention) - ek video mein 10 teams, 2 teams per chunk. Lambai ~1,250 shabd (~8-9 min).
**Video ka dhancha:** Chunk 1 = hook + KKR + LSG | 2 = CSK + MI | 3 = SRH + PBKS | 4 = DC + GT | 5 = RR + RCB + ant.

## Label ka matlab
- OFFICIAL = IPL/BCCI ne announce kiya (wire reports ke zariye dekha; upload se pehle IPL ke official X/site par ek baar dekh lo).
- REPORT = HT ya doosre media ne kaha.
- ANALYSIS = HT ke writer ka andaza ("likely retentions"). Ye **official list nahi hai**. Video mein hamesha "HT ke andaze ke hisab se / hamare hisaab se" bolna.
- RUMOR = sirf charcha.

## FACT PACK

### Context (chunk 1 mein)
F1 (OFFICIAL): Retention aur release list jama karne ki deadline **17 November (kal, Mangalwar)** hai. BCCI ne date aage badhayi thi kyunki pehli date India ke 5th ODI (NZ, 15 Nov) se takra rahi thi. [HT; IANS; Business Today]
F2 (OFFICIAL): Mini-auction **16 December ko Goa** mein hoga. [Tribune; Express Tribune]
F3 (REPORT-HT): IPL 2027 league ka **20th season** hoga.

### KKR
F4 (REPORT-HT): KKR 2026 mein **7th** rahi: 6 jeet, 7 haar, 1 no-result.
F5 (REPORT-HT): Batting mein unka scoring rate **9.26** tha, jo bada kamzor pehlu raha.
F6 (REPORT-HT): **Ajinkya Rahane ke retirement** ke baad captaincy ki jagah khaali hai. List se pehle ye tay karna hoga.
F7 (REPORT-HT): Cameron Green aur Matheesha Pathirana par KKR ne **kul ₹43.20 crore** lagaye hain. Pathirana ka hissa **₹18 crore**.
F8 (REPORT-HT): Pathirana ne pichle season **sirf 8 gend** dali, phir injury se bahar ho gaye.
F9 (ANALYSIS-HT): Team management Green ko **Andre Russell ka replacement** maanti hai. Wo regularly bowling nahi kar rahe the, phir bhi retain hone ke kareeb lagte hain.
F10 (ANALYSIS-HT): KKR ke likely retentions: Narine, Varun Chakaravarthy, Rinku Singh, Angkrish Raghuvanshi, Finn Allen, Harshit Rana. Dekhne wale: Green, Pathirana, fringe overseas players.

### LSG
F11 (REPORT-HT): LSG IPL 2026 mein **aakhri (last)** sthaan par rahi.
F12 (OFFICIAL): Rishabh Pant, jo kabhi IPL ke sabse mehnge player the, **₹15 crore mein DC trade** hue. Kuldeep Yadav DC se **LSG aaye**. LSG ne captain aur poster boy khoya, spin mazboot hua.
F13 (REPORT-HT): Nicholas Pooran ne 2026 mein 14 match mein **234 run**, strike rate lagbhag **128**, ₹21 crore mein. Returns umeed se kam. LSG unhe captaincy ke option mein bhi dekh sakti hai.
F14 (ANALYSIS-HT): LSG ke likely retentions: Mitchell Marsh, Aiden Markram, Ayush Badoni, Kuldeep Yadav, Mayank Yadav. Dekhne wale: Pooran, Avesh Khan, Anrich Nortje, Wanindu Hasaranga, Mohsin Khan.

### CSK
F15 (REPORT-HT): CSK ke **5 titles** hain (MI ke saath sabse zyada), lekin pichle **3 season** league stage se aage nahi gayi. [CHECK: bolne se pehle verify]
F16 (OFFICIAL): Trade mein **Liam Livingstone aur Jack Edwards CSK aaye**, **Khaleel Ahmed aur Nathan Ellis SRH gaye**. Isse CSK ko pace ke liye jagah banani hogi. [HT; Siasat]
F17 (REPORT-HT): CSK ke sabse mehnge do khiladi **Prashant Veer aur Kartik Sharma, dono ₹14.20 crore**. Veer (20 saal, all-rounder) ne bat se do achhi pariyan kheli, gend se kuch nahi. Kartik Sharma ki shuruaat kamzor rahi, baad mein behtar.
F18 (REPORT-HT): MS Dhoni ka 2027 khelna abhi **tay nahi** hai.
F19 (ANALYSIS-HT): CSK ke likely retentions: Ruturaj Gaikwad, Sanju Samson, Shivam Dube, Ayush Mhatre, Dewald Brevis, Noor Ahmad, Jamie Overton, Anshul Kamboj. Dekhne wale: Veer, Kartik Sharma, Matthew Short, Zak Foulkes.

### MI
F20 (OFFICIAL): Hardik Pandya **MI se gaye**, Arshdeep Singh **MI aaye**. [HT; Tribune; Express Tribune/Fanatik: organizers ne announce kiya]
F21 (ANALYSIS-HT): Arshdeep ke aane ke baad MI Trent Boult aur Shardul Thakur ki jagah dobara tolegi.
F22 (ANALYSIS-HT): MI ke likely retentions: Bumrah, Arshdeep, Suryakumar Yadav, Rohit Sharma, Tilak Varma, Naman Dhir, Ryan Rickelton, Deepak Chahar. Dekhne wale: Boult, Shardul, Quinton de Kock.

### SRH
F23 (REPORT-HT): SRH ne 2026 achha kiya aur unki batting sabse mazboot mein se thi. Bowling ke gaps bharne ke liye Khaleel Ahmed, T Natarajan aur Nathan Ellis ko trade kiya.
F24 (REPORT-HT): Harshal Patel se umeed jitni nahi mili, aur trades ke baad bowlers bahut hain.
F25 (REPORT-HT): Captain Pat Cummins ne **IPL 2027 skip karne ka ishara** diya hai. Team ne unpar bahut paisa lagaya hai.
F26 (ANALYSIS-HT): SRH ke likely retentions: Abhishek Sharma, Travis Head, Heinrich Klaasen, Ishan Kishan, Nitish Kumar Reddy, Eshan Malinga. Dekhne wale: Cummins, Harshal.

### PBKS
F27 (OFFICIAL): Window ki **sabse badi trade**: Hardik Pandya PBKS aaye, Arshdeep MI gaye. Hardik ka contract **₹18 crore**. Sawal: squad balance kaise bacha rahega. [HT; Tribune]
F28 (REPORT-HT): Yuzvendra Chahal bhi **₹18 crore** par hain. Unhone **12 wicket** liye, control kabhi-kabhi gaayab dikha. Hardik ke aane se unki jagah par sawal.
F29 (ANALYSIS-HT): PBKS ke likely retentions: Shreyas Iyer, Hardik Pandya, Prabhsimran Singh, Priyansh Arya, Shashank Singh, Nehal Wadhera, Marco Jansen. Dekhne wale: Chahal, Marcus Stoinis, Mitchell Owen, Lockie Ferguson.

### DC
F30 (OFFICIAL): **Rishabh Pant DC lautey**, Kuldeep Yadav LSG gaye. T Natarajan out, **Zeeshan Ansari** in.
F31 (RUMOR): **Axar Patel aur Ashutosh Sharma** ke trade ki charcha thi, lekin DC dono ko **rakhna** chahti hai (HT ke mutabik).
F32 (ANALYSIS-HT): DC ke likely retentions: Pant, KL Rahul, Axar Patel, Tristan Stubbs, Sameer Rizvi, Ashutosh Sharma. Dekhne wale: Mukesh Kumar, Dushmantha Chameera, pace attack ki banawat.

### GT
F33 (REPORT-HT): GT 2026 ki **runner-up** thi, core lagbhag tay.
F34 (RUMOR): **Harshal Patel** GT se jude bataye ja rahe hain. Trade ho ya na ho, GT death bowling ke liye pacers ko jagah de sakti hai.
F35 (REPORT-HT): Shahrukh Khan aur Rahul Tewatia umeed par khare nahi utre. Glenn Phillips par nazar.
F36 (ANALYSIS-HT): GT ke likely retentions: Shubman Gill, Sai Sudharsan, Jos Buttler, Rashid Khan, Mohammed Siraj, Kagiso Rabada, Washington Sundar. Dekhne wale: Shahrukh, Tewatia, Phillips, fringe overseas batting.

### RR
F37 (REPORT-HT): RR ki batting mazboot, lekin 2026 mein bowling batting ke barabar nahi chali.
F38 (REPORT-HT): Shimron Hetmyer par nazar. Tushar Deshpande ne 7 match mein **sirf 4 wicket** liye, unka **₹6.50 crore** ka contract sawalon mein.
F39 (ANALYSIS-HT): RR ke likely retentions: Vaibhav Sooryavanshi, Yashasvi Jaiswal, Dhruv Jurel, Riyan Parag, Ravindra Jadeja, Jofra Archer, Donovan Ferreira. Dekhne wale: Hetmyer, Deshpande, Ravi Bishnoi, mehnge bowling contracts.

### RCB
F40 (REPORT-HT): RCB ne **lagatar do season (2025, 2026)** title jeete. Squad lagbhag tay. Release sirf fringe players ki ho sakti hai.
F41 (ANALYSIS-HT): RCB ke likely retentions: Virat Kohli, Rajat Patidar, Phil Salt, Josh Hazlewood, Bhuvneshwar Kumar, Krunal Pandya, Devdutt Padikkal, Tim David.
