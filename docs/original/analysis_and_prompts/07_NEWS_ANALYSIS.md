## 2. Teen alag "channel style" jo dikhe
1. **Opinion/analysis creator** (5, 6, 11, 12, aur shayad 2/3): "mera dil kya kehta hai", memes, comment-bait, bahut lamba (3,000-5,800 shabd). Brand awaaz par chalta hai.
2. **Daily news show** (15, 16, 17): episode number + date + "aaj ki 10 badi khabar". Lambai 2,200-2,800. Habit banane wala format.
3. **Seedhi short khabar** (1, 7, 8, 9, 14): 600-1,100 shabd. Kam opinion, zyada list.

## 3. Jo sab dete hain (MINIMUM, hamein bhi dena hai)
1. **Hook:** "sabse badi khabar" + sawaal ("kya hoga?"). (1, 6, 9, 12, 15, 16, 17)
2. **Source ka naam:** PTI, Cricbuzz, Business Standard, "reports ke mutabik". (6, 10, 12, 14, 15, 17)
3. **Kaun kahan gaya:** team, player, kis paise mein. (5, 9, 14, 15)
4. **Paise ka hisaab:** price kitna tha, kitne mein trade hua, purse par asar. (11, 14, 15)
5. **Pichle ya agle step ki date:** trade window, release/retention, 16 Dec mini auction (Goa). (14, 16)
6. **Ant mein comment sawaal + subscribe.** (sabme)

## 4. Inme jo cheezein ACHHI lagi (seekhne layak)
- **Script 14 (sabse saaf):** pehle "Official trade list", phir "Rumors". Har trade mein purana price → naya price → nuksaan/faida ("10.75 cr se 5 cr, yaani 5.75 cr ka nuksaan"). Chhota (847 shabd) aur saaf.
- **Script 6:** "Is report par hum kitna trust kar sakte hain?" Phir "mera dil kya kehta hai?" Trust check + apni raay alag-alag. Yehi sabse original cheez hai.
- **Script 12:** "Rumor check" format. Do agencies (PTI vs Cricbuzz) ke aamne-saamne hone ki baat, phir apna nateeja. Bharosa banata hai.
- **Script 5:** har team ke liye numbered "kyun faida / kyun khatra" (Punjab ke liye 1-4, MI ke liye 1-3). Dono taraf dikhati hai.
- **Script 7:** "retain, release ya trade? Teen option" wala watchlist. Simple, chhota.
- **Script 15, 17:** episode number + date + "10 updates" ka wada hook mein hi. Dekhne wale ko pata hota hai kitna chalega.
- **Script 1:** team-wise repeat pattern ("CSK ne 8 release kiye... MI ne 10..."). Sunne mein saaf.
- **Script 13:** "disclaimer deta hoon, ye khabar mujhe sahi nahi lag rahi, bilkul believe mat karna." Seedhi imaandari.
- **Script 8, 9:** "night video mein poora batayenge" jaisa agle video ka wada (par details ke saath ho to).

## 5. Inki KAMZORIYAN (hamein inse behtar banana hai)
1. **Source ke bina "confirm":** script 8 mein "poori tarah confirm ho chuka hai" lekin koi naam nahi. Script 9 mein bhi. Ye misleading hai.
2. **Khabar kam, "update aa rahi hai" zyada:** script 8 mein 628 shabd mein ek bhi pakka naam nahi. "Bade-bade trade", "shockingly bhi release", sirf hype.
3. **Rumor ko fact ki tarah bolna:** script 11 mein "डेफिनेटली रिलीज़ करने वाली है" jaisi lines predictions ke liye. Script 9 mein "confirm ho jao" ek T10 performance par.
4. **Bahut lambe:** 42 minute (script 11), 29 minute (script 6). Ek hi topic baar-baar ghoomta hai.
5. **Subscribe ki bheekh bahut jaldi:** 3-6% par hi (6, 11, 15, 16, 17). Pehle khabar, phir ask.
6. **Ghatiya/abusive bhasha:** script 2/4 mein Pakistan aur bachchon ke liye gaali jaisi baatein, script 3 mein ek gaali, script 10 mein Pakistan par tikhi tippani. YouTube ke hate/harassment rules ke liye khatra. Demonetization ke liye bhi.
7. **Alag-alag topic ek video mein:** script 2/4 mein U19 match + trade + Jaiswal; script 17 mein chhatra andolan/BCCI code of conduct jaise topic.
8. **Date/numbers mein ulta-seedha:** alag scripts mein trade/release ki dates alag dikhti hain (jaise 15 Nov, 17 Nov). Ek source se date check nahi hota.
9. **Podcast promo, dharm/rajneeti ki clip** (10, 13): cricket audience ke liye bekaar, algorithm bhi confuse.
10. **Naam ki galtiyan:** transcript mein bhi, aur shayad bolne mein bhi.

## 6. Hamare 4 News Formats (8-9 min, ~1,250 shabd)

### Format A - "Aaj ki Top 5" (roz ka)
| Hissa | Shabd |
|---|---|
| Hook (aaj ki sabse badi khabar + 5 ka wada) | 100 |
| 5 khabar x ~190 shabd | 950 |
| Aaj ki raay (sabse bada sawaal) + ant | 200 |
Har khabar: **headline → status (OFFICIAL / REPORT / RUMOR) → source → numbers → "kyun zaroori" (1-2 line)**.

### Format B - "Ek badi khabar, poori kahani"
Hook → kya hua → source check (kitna bharosa) → paise ka hisaab → team-wise faida/khatra (2-2) → aage kya (date) → meri raay + risk.
Shabd: 100 / 150 / 150 / 200 / 250 / 120 / 280 = ~1,250.

### Format C - "Release / Retention list"
Team-wise: kitne release, sabse bada naam, "sabse badi surprise", purse par asar. 10 teams ek video mein nahi: 2 parts mein (5+5).

### Format D - "Rumor ya sach?" (5 min)
Rumor → kahan se aaya → kisne mana kiya → mera nateeja: **Pakka / Mumkin / Kamzor / Jhootha**.

**Breaking (3-4 min):** sirf tab jab koi ek pakki baat ho (kaun, kya, source, number). "Kuch bada aa raha hai" jaise bina-details wale video nahi.

## 7. Jo hum EXTRA jodenge (competitors se behtar)
1. **Status label har khabar par:** OFFICIAL / REPORT / RUMOR (screen par rang ke saath).
2. **Source line:** "PTI ke mutabik", "Cricbuzz report" (script 6, 12 se seekha).
3. **Trust meter:** 1-5 mein bharosa + ek line karan (script 6 se, ham screen par dikhayenge).
4. **Paise ka card:** purana price → naya price → purse par asar (script 14 se).
5. **Trade ka winner/loser:** dono teams ke 2-2 point (script 5 se).
6. **Timeline card:** trade window, retention, mini auction (16 Dec, Goa; verify karke).
7. **"Kal kya dekhna hai":** watchlist (script 7 se).
8. **Sudhaar line:** agar pehle ka claim galat nikla to khud bolna ("pichle video mein maine X kaha tha, wo galat nikla"). Competitors ye nahi karte.
9. **Rumor scorecard:** kaun si rumor sahi/galat nikli (hamara, 10 video ke baad).
10. **Daily series ka naam aur episode number** (script 15-17 se), taaki roz dekhne ki aadat bane.
11. **Gaali/communal/personal attack bilkul nahi.** Mazaak sirf khabar ya sthiti par, kisi desh ya insaan par nahi.
12. **"Confirmed" sirf official/do bharose ke source ke baad.**
13. **Ek video = ek hi topic** (ya Top 5 format).
14. **CTA ek baar, ant mein;** pehle 90 second mein subscribe ki bheekh nahi.
15. **[APNI RAAY]** ek jagah: tumhari awaaz mein 1-2 line. Original content isi se banta hai.

## 8. Language style
- Hinglish bolchal, cricket shabd English mein (trade, retain, release, purse, auction).
- Vaakya lambe: 12-22 shabd average (script 7: 22). Hamare liye 10-14.
- Filler: "ऐसी अपडेट निकल के आ रही है" (script 8, 9 mein baar-baar), "भाई", "देखो". Bahut kam rakho.
- Humor (scripts 13, 3): mazedaar jab khabar par ho, bekaar jab gaali/jaati/desh par ho.
