## 2. Red flags (inhe copy NAHI karna)
Tumhara faisla: fantasy/Dream11/betting nahi. In scripts ka bada hissa wahi hai:
- Fantasy: Dream11 team, Captain/VC, "selection %", contest join karna (1, 3, 4, 6, 12).
- Betting-style: "favourite 50-60 paise pe khulegi", "20-30 pe entry", "session pattern", "market", "trading wala match", phone number par "kaam" (5, 10, 11, 8).
- Giveaway: Instagram par 2 lakh ka giveaway, "top 2 comments ko 500" (4, 7, 8, 9).
- Telegram/WhatsApp paid-group jaisa promotion, "jackpot match astrology" (6).
- Script 3 ki shuruaat mein "Online Gaming Act 2025" ka disclaimer hai. Matlab ye niche legal dabav mein hai.
Seekh: ye sab YouTube ke liye bhi risk hai (YPP review mein). Hum sirf cricket analysis rakhenge.

## 3. Jo sab karte hain (MINIMUM, ye hamein bhi dena hai)
1. **Team news hook:** pehle 10-20 second mein "kaun nahi khelega, kaun aayega". (1, 3, 5, 12)
2. **Match ka dav:** series kahan khadi hai, kaun haare to kya hoga. (4, 5, 10, 11)
3. **Pitch report:** average score, highest/lowest, bat-first vs chase record, pace vs spin wickets. (4, 5, 7, 11)
4. **Venue par player stats:** runs, average, wicket. (1, 4)
5. **Head-to-head.** (1, 4, 5, 6, 7, 10)
6. **Recent form aur pichle match ka recap.** (1, 4, 6, 11)
7. **Probable Playing 11 + batting order.** (sabme)
8. **Player-wise baat.** (2, 12)
9. **Verdict: kaun jeetega.** (2, 4, 5, 11)
10. **Ant mein like/subscribe/comment ka CTA.** (sabme)

## 4. Inme jo cheezein ACHHI lagi (seekhne layak)
- **Script 4:** verdict pehle hi bata deta hai ("India comfortably jeetegi"), phir kaaran. Dekhne wale ko ruka rehne ki wajah milti hai.
- **Script 4:** pichle 3-4 match ka pace vs spin table (jaise "Pacers 55 wickets, spinners 9"). Seedha pitch ka nature samajh aata hai.
- **Script 4:** bowling over-rotation ("powerplay mein Duffy, Tickner, Clark"). Kaam ki insight.
- **Script 1:** pitch pehle ke match mein use ho chuki hai (76.4 over). Isse spin ka role badalne ka tark deta hai.
- **Script 2:** har player par 1-2 line ("form hai/nahi, kyun"), tark ke saath. Ek hi tone mein seedhi baat. Is script ka analysis-style sabse original hai.
- **Script 2, 9:** "Maine bola tha X 100 maarega, maara." Apna track record dikhana trust banata hai.
- **Script 3:** "Toss bat first kare to kya, bowl first kare to kya." Do scenario wali soch.
- **Script 10:** ground ki boundary size (55 m seedhi, rugby stadium) aur phase-wise run rate. Alag cheez batati hai.
- **Script 12:** key player nahi khel raha ("Sutherland out") to uska asar kya padega.
- **Script 5, 11:** chhoti lambai (1,300-1,500 shabd) mein pura kaam. Seedha content.

## 5. Inki KAMZORIYAN (hamein inse behtar banana hai)
1. **CTA bahut jaldi aur bahut baar:** Telegram/giveaway pehle 9-23% mein hi aa jaata hai (1: 9%, 2: 18%, 3: 15%, 4: 11%, 8: 18%, 9: 23%). Content se pehle promotion = dekhne wale bhaag jaate hain. Telegram/WhatsApp ka zikr 5 se 17 baar tak hai.
2. **Numbers ki bauchhaar bina context ke:** script 1 mein ek saans mein 10+ player ke runs bol diye jaate hain. Sunne wale ko kuch yaad nahi rehta.
3. **Aapas mein ulta-seedha numbers:** H2H alag-alag scripts mein alag hai (118 match 53-63 / 125 match 65-52-16 NR / 127 match 65-53). Ek hi source se verify nahi hota.
4. **Live galtiyan:** script 6 mein "India lost two, won three... wait, they have won two and lost three." Bharosa kam hota hai.
5. **Bade-bade daave:** "poore YouTube par koi nahi batayega", "naam hi kaafi hai". Proof nahi, khokhla lagta hai.
6. **Faltu baatein:** thailand trip, shareer/gora karne ki baat (script 7), audience ko daantna ("tum log mehnat nahi karte", script 9). Retention girti hai.
7. **Bahut lambe:** 15-27 minute, beech mein kahani bikharti hai.
8. **Predictions ki jawabdehi nahi:** hits ka dhol peetna, misses chhupana (script 8 mein haar ka bahana).
9. **Rumor/confirmed ka fark nahi:** Playing 11 ke "mujhe update mila hai" jaisi lines bina label ke.
10. **Voice/format ek jaisa:** har video lagbhag ek jaisa dikhta hai, repetitive jaisa.

## 6. Hamara BEHTAR format (8-9 min, ~1,250 shabd)
| Samay | Hissa | Shabd | Kya |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00-0:30 | Hook + verdict teaser | ~70 | Sabse bada team-news ya twist + "aaj meri raay: X" (poora tark baad mein) |
| 0:30-1:15 | Match ka dav | ~100 | Series ka sach, haare to kya hoga |
| 1:15-2:45 | Pitch aur conditions | ~200 | Avg score, pace vs spin table, bat-first/chase, pitch reuse, mausam/dew, timing |
| 2:45-4:00 | Form, H2H, pichla match | ~170 | Sirf 3-4 numbers, card par dikhao |
| 4:00-5:30 | Playing 11 + key players | ~220 | Confirmed/Rumor label ke saath |
| 5:30-6:30 | Phase aur matchup | ~150 | Powerplay/middle/death, 2-3 bowler vs batter jodiyan |
| 6:30-7:30 | Toss scenario | ~130 | Bat first to kya, chase to kya |
| 7:30-8:30 | Verdict + risk + [APNI RAAY] | ~150 | Kaaran, "kya galat ho sakta hai", meri raay |
| 8:30-9:00 | Ant | ~60 | Ek poll sawaal + subscribe |

## 7. Jo hum EXTRA jodenge (competitors se behtar)
1. **Verdict pehle, kaaran baad mein** (script 4 se seekha, 3 kaaran ke saath).
2. **"Kya galat ho sakta hai" line:** apni raay ka khatra khud batana. Imaandari = bharosa.
3. **Apna scorecard:** har video mein pichli prediction sahi/galat, dono dikhao. Hits ke saath misses bhi. (Shuruaat mein ye khaali hoga, isliye pehle 10 video baad se.)
4. **Factor scorecard:** form, pitch fit, bowling depth, batting depth, venue record. Har factor par kaun aage, phir verdict. Ye odds/paise nahi hai, sirf cricket ke karan.
5. **Toss scenario block** (script 3 se seekha, bina fantasy ke).
6. **2-3 matchup:** jaise "Jamieson vs Rohit". Sirf data ho to.
7. **Pitch reuse + pace/spin table** (script 1, 4 se).
8. **Over-rotation aur phase plan** (script 4 se).
9. **Weather, dew, match ka time (IST)** ek line mein. Script 3 ne sirf alarm ki baat ki thi.
10. **Confirmed / Rumor label** har team news par.
11. **Ek hi source, ek date:** har number ke saath source aur date Fact Pack mein. Number ek saath 3 se zyada ek saans mein nahi.
12. **Cards ke saath:** pitch card, comparison card, scorecard card. Photo ke bina. Numbers screen par, awaaz mein sirf kahani.
13. **Chapters/timestamps** description mein.
14. **Comment poll:** "Aap kise jitaoge?" CTA sirf ant mein, aur pehle 90 second ke baad ek chhota.
15. **Apni awaaz ka ek hissa [APNI RAAY]:** ye original content banata hai.

## 8. Language style jo sabme mila
- Hinglish bolchal ki bhasha; cricket shabd English mein (wicket, powerplay, death).
- Filler baar-baar: "ठीक है?" (script 3: 23, script 8: 29), "भाई", "देखो", "है ना".
- Seedhe sawal ("तो क्या इंडिया चलेगी?") script 2 mein khoob.
- Hamare liye: chhote vaakya (10-14 shabd), filler kam, ek vaakya ek baat. Script 12 (21 shabd/vaakya) aur script 2 (22) bahut lambe vaakya wale hain, bolne mein thakaate hain.
